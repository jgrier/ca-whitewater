II

Class II Classics

Tier 1 — Iconic / Must-Do
  • Lower American — Nimbus to Discovery Park
    I-II · 23 mi
    Sacramento's home river. Most-paddled water in the region. Year-round flows from Folsom Dam. Where every Sac-area paddler learns. mellow II
  • East Fork Carson — Overnight
    II · 20 mi
    II with III pulses. Hot springs, desert canyon, multi-day wilderness. The overnight experience is fundamentally Class II in character. sporty II
  • Klamath — Iron Gate to Sarah Totten
    II · 48 mi
    Premier NorCal multi-day float. Big-volume volcanic canyon, excellent camping, 2–4 day trip. Accessible to open canoes. mellow II
  • Trinity — Lewiston to Junction City
    II · 30 mi
    Dam-released, reliable spring–fall. Gorgeous scenery, good camping. A few III rapids keep it interesting. Best "intro to river camping" in NorCal. mellow II
  • Mokelumne — Electra Run
    II · 6 mi
    Class II at normal flows, II+/III above 1500 cfs. Closest dam-released run to Sacramento/Rocklin. The default warmup or bring-a-friend run. mellow II
  • Lower Kern — Town Run
    II-III · 7 mi
    Isabella to Sandy Flat. SoCal's most accessible dam-released whitewater. Summer staple. Predominantly II with a few III drops. sporty II
  • Sacramento — Redding to Anderson
    II · 12 mi
    Big-volume tailwater below Shasta Dam. Famous trout water. Cold, clear, year-round flows. mellow II
  • Russian River — Cloverdale to Healdsburg
    II · 25 mi
    Bay Area's winter/spring river trip. Wine country scenery, mild rapids. Good for canoes and kayaks. Rain-dependent. mellow II
Tier 2 — Regional Classics
  • Stanislaus — Camp Nine to Parrotts Ferry
    II · 9 mi
    Gold Country limestone canyon. Dam-controlled, clear water. Popular with families and canoeists. mellow II
  • Trinity — Junction City to Burnt Ranch
    II-III · 30 mi
    Extends the Lewiston float downstream. Mostly II before canyon steepens. Multi-day extension. sporty II
  • Klamath — Sarah Totten to Happy Camp
    II-III · 36 mi
    Continuation of Iron Gate float. Picks up slightly — transitions from II to borderline III. Multi-day. sporty II
  • Truckee — Tahoe City to River Ranch
    II · 5 mi
    Short, scenic, convenient. Default "quick paddle near Tahoe." Summer dam releases. mellow II
  • Cache Creek — Upper (Hwy 20 to Bear Creek)
    II(III) · 18 mi
    Calmer section above Rumsey Canyon. Winter/spring, rain-dependent. Sac/Bay Area within-reach. sporty II
  • Smith River — Jed Smith to Mouth
    I-II · 5 mi
    The clearest water in California. Worth paddling for scenery alone. Winter/spring. mellow II
  • Cosumnes River — Michigan Bar to Hwy 99
    II · 12 mi
    Sacramento foothills rain-dependent. Narrow, tree-lined, intimate. Short season but very close to Rocklin. II with teeth
  • SF Eel — Leggett to Piercy
    II · 15 mi
    Mellow section of the South Fork Eel. Redwood scenery. Rain-dependent, winter/spring. mellow II

  • Putah Creek — Below Monticello Dam

  • West Carson — Hwy 4 to Woodfords

  • Owens River — Pleasant Valley to Bishop

    Remote. Mostly II with some III. Pretty canyon. sporty II
  • Van Duzen — Grizzly Creek

    North Coast rain-dependent. Short, fun. sporty II
  • Walker River — Main Stem
    II · 8 mi
    Eastern Sierra. Less-known, mellow. mellow II
  • Feather — Oroville to Thermalito
    I-II · 5 mi
    Flatwater-ish but accessible. mellow II
III

Class III Classics

Tier 1 — Iconic / Must-Do
  • South Fork American
    III+ · 15 mi
    Chili Bar, Coloma-to-Lotus, and The Gorge. Most-paddled III in California. Dam-released May–Sep. Meatgrinder, Troublemaker, Satan's Cesspool, Hospital Bar.
  • Middle Fork American — Oxbow to Greenwood
    II-IV · 17 mi
    Continuous III with Tunnel Chute (IV) at mile 1. Dam-released, summer-friendly. Gorgeous canyon.
  • Upper Sacramento — Box Canyon to Sims
    III-IV · 22 mi
    Granite pool-drop under Castle Crags. Spring/early summer. Three sections combinable.
  • East Fork Carson — Overnight
    II+ · 20 mi
    II+ with III pulses, hot springs, multi-day wilderness feel. The classic Sierra overnight.
  • Truckee River — Floriston
    III · 8 mi
    Roadside, reliable, continuous read-and-run Class III.
  • Kings River — Banzai
    III-IV · 9.5 mi
    Garnet Dike to Kirch Flat. Spring snowmelt Sierra classic in a warm canyon.
  • Klamath — Hell's Corner
    III+ · 18 mi
    Big-water III at Happy Camp. Multi-day option. IV at high flow.
  • Cache Creek — Rumsey Canyon
    III · 9 mi
    Sacramento/Bay Area winter-spring staple.
  • Trinity — Pigeon Point
    II-III · 24 mi
    Long season via Lewiston release. Scenic pool-drop III.
  • Lower Kern — Jungle Run
    III/III+ · 7 mi
    Keysville to Miracle Hot Springs. SoCal's summer III classic, dam-controlled. Below Miracle is IV with V+ portage — different run.
Tier 2 — Regional Classics
  • McCloud — Ah-Di-Na
    III-IV · 24 mi
    Remote wilderness run. Reservoir to Shasta.
  • Cal Salmon — South Fork
    III · 6.5 mi
    Methodist Creek to Forks. The III section in an otherwise IV-V area.
  • South Fork Eel — Big Bend to Piercy
    II-III · 21 mi
    Winter/spring. Redwoods scenery.
  • Main Eel — Dos Rios to Alderpoint
    II-IV · 47 mi
    Multi-day wilderness. Rain-dependent.
  • NF Feather — Rock Creek
    III · 8 mi
    The genuine Class III NF Feather run. Dam-released summer/fall. One IV− rapid, easily scouted.
  • Merced — El Portal to Briceburg
    III+ to IV+
    Snowmelt. Rating drifts significantly with CFS. Contains Class V Ned's Gulch.
  • Upper Kern — Camp 3 / Limestone
    III-V · 17 mi
    Genuine III segments exist but you must pick-and-choose. Not read-and-run III.
  • Mokelumne — Electra Run
    II-III · 6 mi
    Class II at normal flows, III only above ~1500 cfs. Closest dam-released run to Sacramento.

  • NF American — Shirttail

    Short, more technical.
  • Arroyo Seco

    Coast Range treat. Narrow season.
  • Little Sur River

    Tiny coastal classic.

    Winter rain runs, Mendocino.

Class IV Classics

Tier 1 — Iconic / Must-Do
  • NF American — Chamberlain Falls
    IV · 5 mi
    The canonical CA "first Class IV." Pool-drop. Slaughter's Sluice, Chamberlain Falls IV+, Zig-Zag. Spring/early-summer snowmelt. sport IV
  • MF American — Oxbow to Greenwood
    II-IV(V+) · 17 mi
    Tunnel Chute (IV) and Kanaka Falls (IV+). Dam-released, summer. The III paddler's step-up. sport IV
  • Tuolumne Main — Meral's Pool to Ward's Ferry
    IV+ · 18 mi
    The California Class IV overnight. Sustained IV, Clavey Falls IV+. Permit required. big-water IV
  • Upper Kern — Thunder Run / Limestone
    IV · 17 mi
    Johnsondale to PH #3. Summer dam-released. Limestone, Joe's Diner, Cable, Buzzard's Perch. IV at 1500–2500 cfs. sport IV
  • Lower Kern — Miracle to Democrat
    IV · 12 mi
    Dam-released summer. Dead Man's Curve, Horseshoe Falls. Mandatory V+ portage at Royal Flush. big-water IV
  • Kaweah — Upper 6 / Gateway
    IV · 3 mi
    Gateway, Chute, Osterizer, Suicide Falls. Technical boulder-garden. Snowmelt spring. creeky IV
  • Kings — Banzai
    III-IV · 9.5 mi
    Fang Tooth IV+, Mule Rock IV, Banzai IV+. Spring snowmelt, warm canyon. sport IV
  • Upper Sac — Box Canyon to Gibson

  • Goodwin Canyon — Stanislaus
    IV+ · 4 mi

  • Klamath Hell's Corner / Trinity at High Water
    IV · 18 mi
    Hell's Corner at 2500+ cfs becomes genuine IV. Big-water day trips. big-water IV

V

Class V Classics

Tier 1 — The Bucket List
  • Cherry Creek / Upper Tuolumne
    V to V+ · 9 mi
    Almost certainly the most famous V run in the world. 12 V rapids, Miracle Mile, Mushroom, Flat Rock Falls, Lumsden Falls. Dam-released mid-July–Labor Day. solid V
  • Forks of the Kern
    V · 17 mi
    40 Class IV rapids with 3 mandatory Vs (Vortex, Westwall, Carson Falls). Wilderness, 2-mile hike-in, Golden Trout. 1–3 days. solid V
  • MF Feather — Devil's Canyon
    V · 32.5 mi
    "The best self-supported wilderness trip in California" — Holbek/Stanley. 3–4 day expedition. Granite Dome Falls portage. solid V expedition
  • MF Feather — Bald Rock Canyon
    V+ · 6.5 mi
    Granite canyon day trip. Mandatory portage at Atom Bomb Falls. Rating disputed — V+ in books, IV+/V per McQuoid. stout V/V+
  • Burnt Ranch Gorge — Trinity
    V · 10 mi
    IV+ with 4 mandatory V rapids (Burnt Ranch Falls series). Dam-released summer/fall. solid V
  • Cal Salmon — Nordheimer
    V · 10.2 mi
    IV+ with 3 Vs: Cascade, Last Chance, Freight Train. Often the first "real V" for advancing paddlers. Spring snowmelt. solid V
  • Giant Gap — NF American
    IV-V · 14.5 mi
    Hike-in, deep canyon, 3 V rapids. Spring snowmelt. Classic wilderness day trip. solid V
  • Fordyce Creek
    V · 7–12 mi
    Dam-released, high-Sierra granite. Fordyce Falls, Split Falls, The Hole that Ate the Donner Party. solid V
  • Kaweah — Hospital Rock
    V · 5 mi
    V-Slide, Zero to Sixty. Granite bedrock and slides in Sequoia NP. Short season. solid V
  • Dinkey Creek — Waterfalls
    V+ · 6.3 mi
    "Arguably the best whitewater run in California" — Adventure Sports Journal. 2-day expedition. stout V+
